## 2. Understanding DORA

Reviewing the **DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act)** regulation, I came to better understand its high importance for the financial institutions within the EU. 

It establishes well structured requirements across five key pillars, guiding financial organizations on an effective management of the Information and Communication Technology (ICT) risks, third-party entities, incident response and more.

DORA includes (but doesn`t limit to) defeat, respond or recover from the cyber threats.

### 5. DORA Compliance Framework at ING Bank:

ING Bank is committed to ensuring its operations are aligned with all the 5 pillars of DORA.

After completing the DORA Assessment Book [link to Workbook](https://github.com/emilia4cyber/cybersecurity-portfolio/blob/DORA-gap-analysis-report/DORA%20Assessment%20Workbook-%20ING.pdf), I came to the following conclusions:

 **1.ICT Risk Management**
- ING has a strong developed ICT risk management framework, aligned with DORA, NIS2, and ISO 27001.
- ING has a clear risk ownership across departments, using tools like SIEM (Splunk) to manage risks better.
*Area of improvement: to increase the use of automation more specially in 3rd party risk asessments.

**2.ICT Related Incident Response**
- ING claims that their SOC teams operate 24/7 and use automated tools for detecting, classifying and escalating threats.
*Area of improvement: Post incident lessons process to be improved through more simulations trainings.

**3.Digital Operational Resilience Testing**
 - ING’s disaster recovery (DR) testing and business continuity planning (BCP) are well-documented and periodically tested.
- ING performs regularly tabletop exercises for improvement.
*Area of improvement: to expand the testing and use more automation.

**4.ICT 3rd Party Risk**
- ING performs strict due diligence & security assessments for third-party vendors and suppliers.
- ING has clear contractual security clauses ( as per DORA requirements) in the third party agreements.

**5.Information Sharing**
- ING has an active membership within FS-ISAC, a cyber intelligence sharing platform.
- ING uses automated threat intelligence feeds for better management of the cyber threats.

  ## 6. Future recommendations:
- ING should remain vigilant as cyber threats keep developing in the changing environment, and to keep  an eye on other **EU financial regulations** outside of DORA (like GDPR updates).
- To improve threat detection and response times, **AI-driven security analytics** should be implemented in more cases/scenarios.
- ING could increase its participation in **cyber intelligence-sharing networks** in order to remain ahead of new threats.
